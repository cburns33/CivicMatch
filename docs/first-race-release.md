## Public experience

- `/` lists the three printed candidates and four certified write-ins using the August 28 state certifications, with separate write-in labels and original-source links. It makes no claim about an individual's complete ballot.
- Seven versioned questions cover private-school funding, property-tax replacement, institutional homebuyers, adult cannabis legalization, abortion access, capital punishment, and test-based school accountability. Two questions concern education; this selection does not represent every office-relevant issue.
- Answers distinguish neutral, skipped, and untouched states. Importance is independent for each question. Answer review precedes comparisons. Users can edit, clear, and print.
- Answers persist only in versioned browser localStorage. Unknown revisions and invalid saved answers are dropped. Storage failure keeps the current in-memory visit usable and displays a message. No political answers are sent to the server.
- Weighted distance uses the existing method v1 and weights 0.5/0.75/1/1.5/2, with unrated importance at 1. Headline agreement needs five applicable answers, five supported mappings, and 60% comparison coverage. Missing evidence yields insufficient information, with no zero-percent substitute.
- Research and comparison coverage, weighted coverage, included topics, equal-weight sensitivity, and a separately gated high-priority subset are shown. Candidates retain certification order and receive no ranking. A count exposes the questions shared across all seven candidates.
- The user clarified that this is for the user and friends and authorized sourced provisional interpretations without mandatory human approval. Provisional mappings contribute to comparisons with an explicit AI-assisted label. Unresolved and limited evidence stay unscored. An `approved` label still requires named human review and a date; the release claims no such review.
- `/demo` retains the prior synthetic Austin/Houston explorer. `/methodology` describes the selected-race implementation and evidence standard.

## Private research boundary

The original brief required human approval before publication. The user's October 27 clarification supersedes that requirement for this selected-race small-group version. Public mappings are labeled provisional. Source verification, interpretation, uncertainty, and human approval remain distinct concepts. Existing county composition publication guards are unchanged.

Migration `20261027133346_race_guide_review_preview.sql` adds one immutable RLS-protected draft-packet table. Only the trusted database operator can stage pending versions. Current provisioned reviewers can read. Anonymous and ordinary signed-in users cannot read draft packets; clients and service_role cannot stage or mutate them. Checksums are generated by the database on insertion. The table cannot record approved research.

`/admin` offers a private governor research preview after reviewer sign-in. Its API validates the bearer token with Auth, checks current database reviewer membership, and reads the pinned draft through that user's RLS. It has no public draft query parameter, approval action, or publisher. Responses use private/no-store caching. Sign-out unmounts the preview. The preview calculates proposed coverage and agreement with an explicit pending-review label; public calculations reject pending packets.

Raw archived pages and pending working drafts remain in ignored storage and the private database. The authorized public provisional packet is `src/data/governor-research.json`, parsed with Zod and versioned in Git. It has 15 mapped positions across 15 primary sources, five supported questions for each printed candidate, and two explicit exclusions. All qualified write-ins remain visible with missing coverage. Public packet metadata retains source URLs, byte checksums, retrieval dates, known publication dates, policy rationale, and limitations. No party-based mappings, insider interpretations, or inferred motives enter scores.

`scripts/archive-governor-research.mjs` archives small public pages and writes provenance receipts to the ignored store. `scripts/stage-governor-draft.mjs` verifies archived bytes before staging pending v2 through the pinned project's trusted database operator. CLI output is captured to ignored files. Neither script records human approval. Earlier private v1 is preserved.

The user approved all public candidate research and proposed rows. The initial research distinguishes Dixon's quoted voter questions from his own answers, Hinojosa's personal views from her intended governing policy, and replacing STAAR from ending statewide test-based accountability. These distinctions are visible in the source-linked explanation.
